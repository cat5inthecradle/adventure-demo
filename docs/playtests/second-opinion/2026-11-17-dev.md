## What looked broken: confidence and reproduction

1. **Confirmed unexpected behavior, high confidence:** Level 4, Improvise, fresh Hollow House Entrance Hall, type `fly to the moon`. The inspected output selected listen and the world advanced a turn. This violates the request even though it does not grant impossible flight. Model output can vary; this is an observed execution, not a claim that every run repeats it.
2. **Confirmed validation weakness, high confidence:** Level 6, select Invent for all scenarios and type `banana spaceship pizza dinosaur skateboard` in each reason. Continue enables and the card contains those reasons. It demonstrates formal completion without meaningful reflection, rather than a software crash.
3. **Confirmed bypass, intent uncertain:** Next permits skipped activities with disabled Continue, and the card is available with three skipped levels. The striped progress and explicit ending warning suggest the bypass is deliberate; its classroom effect remains a concern.
4. **Confirmed reset behavior, defect status uncertain:** Level 4 reload loses the terminal/dial/model selection and unsaved reason while other lesson progress and world survive. Level 1/world changes reset terminal state without clearing authored answers. The inconsistent scope needs clearer communication; no storage implementation was inspected.

## What Dev would say he learned

“The game has rules and the AI sometimes guesses commands, but I can still finish by typing random words.” That partly matches the intended lesson about interpretation versus authored truth. The successful flight-to-listen substitution weakens the cleaner claim that the game reliably enforces what the player actually requested. Skipping the first two levels and submitting junk means the ending alone does not demonstrate understanding.

## Top five fixes, most valuable first

1. Reject or clarify unrelated action substitutions such as flight becoming listen; make the inspector distinguish interpreted intent from a fallback action actually executed.
2. Make meaningful completion visibly different from reaching the end. Keep navigation available, but make the card's skipped activities prominent enough for a teacher to assess.
3. Strengthen reflection scaffolding so unrelated unique words do not count as a reason; give specific feedback about what the explanation must connect.
4. Explain what reload, returning to Level 1, and changing worlds preserve or reset; preserve useful in-progress settings where appropriate.
5. Replace generic reason errors with clear criteria and keep action/AI authorship feedback short and visible without relying entirely on inspector clicks.
