# Playtest: "Dev", the skimmer (2026-11-16)

Port 5182, Learn view, `hoai-adventure`, viewport 1280x800. Played as Dev (16, skims, clicks through, tries to break things). No source or lesson JSON read. Model for levels 3-4: Qwen2.5 0.5B, which loaded in about 20 seconds (probably cached, so a first load will be slower; I did not see a cold download).

## Conclusion

Dev gets through all six levels and the "My AI rules" card in well under an hour. Five of the six levels (everything except Level 2) can be passed without doing what they ask. He would not stay engaged for the hour. The two best moments (Level 2, where he types "examine coffee mug" and sees it fail, and Level 4, where the AI makes up a mug) are the only places something surprising happens. Level 1, the part he would enjoy, is slow and had a first-command dead end, so he skips it. Every command takes several seconds to answer. He would check out at Level 1 (waiting and a verb that doesn't work), and again at Level 4 (a wall of text with the dial far below the steps). The first time through, he would finish the lesson having learned very little.

## Verdict

Would Dev stay engaged? Only in short bursts. Where he checks out:

1. **Level 1.** Every command shows "processing…" for about 5-10 seconds, even plain parser commands with the interpreter off (`help`, `use clock`, `inventory`). The Hollow House goal text says "Wind the stopped clock", but `wind clock` answers "I don't know the word 'wind'", and `use clock` answers "Nothing happens." A kid who follows the lesson's own words hits a dead end on his first real command. (I did not manage to finish Level 1 in either Drift or Hollow House. See "What I could not test".)
2. **Level 4.** A long scrolling instruction list (6 steps), with the dial itself at the very bottom of the same panel. He never scrolls down to it.
3. **Level 5.** Continue stays greyed out with no explanation when he does the "wrong" thing.

## Things that broke or behaved oddly

### Definitely a bug or a design flaw (I reproduced it)

1. **Level 4 passes with zero engagement.**
   - Steps: go to Level 4, scroll the left panel to the bottom, click **Save my setting** without touching anything.
   - Happened: "Your AI boundary is set. Be ready to explain it." and Continue enables.
   - Expected: at least a changed dial and a reason before it counts.
2. **Level 3's pass message does not match what it checked.**
   - Steps: turn on a model, type `yo can u pick up that crowbar thing`, then `grab the prybar please`.
   - Happened: "You saw the AI translate and admit uncertainty." appeared after the second command. I never asked for something impossible (the sandwich/door prompt).
   - Expected: the pass should need what the text says (one request understood, one clarification).
3. **Duplicate names give an unusable question.**
   - Steps: Level 5, add a thing, then rename it to `prybar` (an existing item) and Save changes. In the game type `examine prybar`.
   - Happened: Save said "Saved." and the game asked "Which do you mean: prybar or prybar?". `inventory` then said "You are carrying prybar." and the player cannot tell the two apart.
   - Expected: block or warn on a name that matches something already in the room.
4. **Level 1 has a dead-end verb in its own goal text.**
   - Steps: Hollow House, Level 1, type `wind clock`.
   - Happened: "I don't know the word 'wind'. Type 'help' for commands." `use clock` gives "Nothing happens."
   - Expected: the verb in the goal ("Wind the stopped clock") works, or the hint names the real command. (Drift's `talk to droid` hint does not apply in the first room, and I did not get further.)
5. **Level 1 can be skipped and the lesson still ends in success.**
   - Steps: Level 1, click **Next →** straight away. Complete 2-6. Card appears and "You finished the lesson."
   - Expected: "Next →" and the progress dot should not mark an unfinished level as done. Dot 1 turned teal even though Level 1 was never passed.
6. **Level 5 passes without checking what you wrote.**
   - Steps: add a "banana cannon" described as "a cannon that fires bananas lol"; take it; rename it "coffee mug"; `examine mug`.
   - Happened: "The mug is real now: it is in the rules, not only in the words." The mug's description says banana cannon. The inspector still shows the internal id `banana_cannon`.
   - Expected: a check that the description is about a mug, or at least a mention that renaming does not rename the thing in the rules.
7. **Level 6 banner and card report the live dial, not the saved one.**
   - Steps: save the dial as Understand in Level 4, then switch to Fill gaps without saving and play. At Level 6 the banner says "In your game, you set the dial to Fill gaps." The card says "I set the AI dial to Fill gaps" with a blank "Why:" line.
   - Expected: the saved setting (Understand), or a note that it was not saved.
8. **Switching worlds does not warn, and the Level 6 banner survives it.**
   - Steps: finish Drift through Level 6, go back to Level 1, click Hollow House.
   - Happened: no confirmation. Level 5 resets for the new world (a "faded portrait" thing), but Level 6 still says "In your game, you set the dial to Fill gaps." even though nothing was set in Hollow House. Lesson progress dots stay filled.
9. **Every command shows "processing…" for several seconds**, including interpreter-off parser commands. I did not time it precisely; I saw it on `examine coffee mug`, `use clock`, and `inventory` each taking longer than the 4-5 s I waited. "Esc to skip" appears but a student is not told what it does. This is the single biggest source of boredom.

### Might be me (not sure it is a bug)

1. At 1400x900 my screenshots cut off the right-hand "How it was understood" panel (the page seemed wider than the capture). At 1280x800 everything showed. Not an app issue, but a classroom screen narrower than about 1280 will show the "bigger screen" note.
2. After a reload on Level 5 the command box is not focused; I had to click it. The saved mug carried over after reload, but the pass was lost until I examined it again.
3. After "Add it to the world", the whole room description is printed again ("The world has changed. Your game carries on from where you were."), which pushes the transcript down. Not a bug, but noisy.
4. Empty form: the "When a player examines it, say" box has no inline error. The browser shows its own "Please fill out this field." bubble (I saw it once; my first screenshot missed it). That worked, but it is not styled like the rest of the app.
5. The Interpreter menu shows percentages ("36%", "80%") with no explanation of what they measure. Dev picked "fastest" and got a model that misread "pick up that crowbar thing" as "look closely at the prybar". That was funny, but nothing on screen said the small model is the reason.
6. In Level 5 the terminal input row drifted up (empty space under it) after several long outputs. It went back to normal after a reload. I only saw it once.

## Can the lesson be passed without learning anything?

Yes, and easily. In the order I'd do it as a skimmer:

1. Level 1: click **Next →**.
2. Level 2: type `examine coffee mug` and click the typed line (the only step that needs attention, and it is the best one).
3. Level 3: pick any model, type any two commands (one wrong is fine).
4. Level 4: scroll to the bottom, click **Save my setting**.
5. Level 5: add the name "coffee mug" with any description, `examine mug`.
6. Level 6: click any dial per app, no reasons.
7. Card: print it with blank "Why:" lines.

The lesson never asks Dev to explain or defend a choice in a way that is checked.

## What Dev says he learned

Honest answer in Dev's voice: "Mostly nothing new. I already knew chatbots make stuff up. The only thing that surprised me was that the game had its own rules and the AI was just guessing words on top. When I typed `examine the mug` with Fill gaps on, it said 'A dark navigation mug waits south.' and then I looked in the panel and it said the AI wrote that and the world didn't change. Okay, that was kind of cool. And I couldn't actually pick up the mug until I made it myself in the form. That's the one thing I'd tell a friend." Everything else (Level 3's model, Level 6's three apps) did not land because he clicked through.

## Top five fixes (most valuable first)

1. **Stop commands from taking 5-10 seconds when no AI is involved** (and tell the student when the AI is the reason). The wait kills the "play" in "Play and Look Inside".
2. **Make each Continue check match what the text asks.** Level 4 needs a changed dial plus a reason; Level 3 needs a real impossible request; Level 5 should read the description; Level 1 must be passed, not skipped. Today every level except Level 2 can be passed without doing the task.
3. **Fix the Level 1 dead end.** The goal verb ("wind") should work, or the hint should name the real command and point at `help`. Also show a one-line reason next to a greyed Continue.
4. **Rework Level 4's layout.** Put the AI dial at the top, show one step at a time, and make "Save my setting" require a reason.
5. **Tighten the Level 5/6 state handling.** Warn on duplicate names; make the Level 6 banner and the card report the saved dial; ask before switching worlds when progress exists; require reasons (or flag blanks on the card) so the card says something.
